# Jaisimha Design Studio

Create a modern, creative, visually impressive personal portfolio website for Jaisimha, a Product Designer and Graphic Designer with 3+ years of professional experience at VMR Graphics, Amberpet.
The website should present Jaisimha as a creative professional specializing in product/packaging design, label design, logo & branding, visiting card design, brochure & flyer design, posters, and print design.
The overall experience should feel creative, modern, premium, professional, and visually driven, with the actual design work being the primary focus.
1. PERSONAL PROFILE / HERO INTRODUCTION
The Home/Hero section should immediately communicate who Jaisimha is and what he does.
Display a large, professional profile picture of Jaisimha prominently in the Hero section. The image should have a modern presentation such as a rounded frame, elegant border, subtle glow, creative shape, or sophisticated editorial layout.
Hero content:
Jaisimha
Product Designer & Graphic Designer
Supporting headline:
"Creative Product & Graphic Designer specializing in packaging, labels, branding, and print design."
Include a short introduction emphasizing his practical design experience and ability to create professional visual identities and print-ready designs for businesses.
Add prominent CTA buttons:
View My Work
Let's Work Together
Add subtle motion/animation to the Hero section without making it distracting.
